	\subsection{Pick's theorem}
		Lattice polygon: $A = I + B/2 - 1$ ($I$ interior, $B$ boundary lattice points;
		segment $(x_1,y_1)$--$(x_2,y_2)$ has $\gcd(|\Delta x|,|\Delta y|)+1$ lattice points).
		Planar graph (Euler): $V - E + F = 1 + C$ ($C$ = \#components, counting the outer face).
